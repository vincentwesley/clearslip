    About Clearslip

    Clearslip blocks ads and trackers using the EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists plus Clearslip's own small Singapore list, and shows what it blocked on each page. It collects nothing and makes no network calls.

    Filter rules: The EasyList authors (easylist.to), CC BY-SA 3.0, for EasyList and EasyPrivacy. The Singapore list is by Vincent Wesley, MIT.
